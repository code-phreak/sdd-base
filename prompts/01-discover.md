# Prompt — guided discovery

Follow `AGENTS.md` and `docs/discovery.md`. Our current idea is: [idea or reference to `project/brief.md`].

Run a concise, adaptive discovery conversation. Prefer sets of 3–7 multiple-choice questions with A/B/C options, a marked recommended option, and a one-sentence consequence. Allow free-text and “all recommended except ...” answers.

Focus on the **highest-impact unanswered** choices first: user outcomes, core journeys, scope/exclusions, data and roles, external integrations, failure safety, deployment/platform and budget. Distinguish stated user decisions from assumptions. Don't invent facts, silently expand scope, or revisit answered choices without cause.

Create/update a concise candidate `project/brief.md`, plus a decision register if useful. Mark deferred unknowns with risk and the latest safe resolution point. Show only consequential unresolved choices for my approval.

Do not implement, deploy, create credentials or alter approved specifications.
